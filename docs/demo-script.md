# Demo script

About three minutes, recorded against the deployed app.

1. Sign in. Say who this is for: the HR manager of a 10,000-person company that kept salaries in spreadsheets.
2. People. Search a name, add the India filter, sort by hire date. Point at the address bar: the view is a link that can be shared.
3. Open a person and show their salary timeline.
4. Change their salary with today's date and a reason. Show the new row and the percentage.
5. Add a person. Pick a country and show the salary label switch currency.
6. Insights. Read the company tiles, then switch between country, department and job title. Say the figures are USD at fixed rates.
7. Mark someone as left and show the headcount drop by one.
8. Close with what is left out and why: payroll, bonuses, import, roles, live rates and questions in plain English.
